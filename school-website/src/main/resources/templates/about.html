Our Story

From a community space to a leading school in Laikipia County

Humble Beginnings

Mt Angels traces its roots to a recreational center in Nyahururu town. Following a national directive to transform such spaces into community amenities, the site was converted into a nursery school in the 1990s — planting the first seeds of what is today a 1,500-pupil institution.

The school was initially run by the Nyahururu Municipal Council. As costs rose, parents working at Nyahururu market stepped in and, through public participation, took over its management. They formed the first School Management Committee — among them Mr. Wanyoike, Mr. Warugongo, and Mr. Muchiri — whose leadership kept the doors open.

In 1999, the school grew into Standard 1 and welcomed its first government-posted teacher, Madam Mercy Ngatia, who served as both teacher and headteacher. That same period, the school land faced a serious threat when interests in the municipality moved to subdivide it for private development. The matter went to court, where Lawyer Mirugi Kariuki of Nakuru successfully defended the school's land — a turning point that secured the school's future.

Through limited but determined resources, parents built classrooms and sustained a lunch programme that helped fund infrastructure. When Free Primary Education was introduced in 2003, the community protected the feeding programme, and it continues to nourish learners to this day.

Mt Angels Today

Mt Angels is now recognised as a leading provider of Primary and Junior Secondary education in Laikipia County. The school serves over 1,500 learners from ECDE through to JSS, supported by a highly motivated teaching team.

Our learners have consistently posted strong results in national assessments — from KCPE in past years to today's Competency Based Education (CBE) assessments — reflecting a culture of academic discipline grounded in our motto: Working Hard For Excellence.

Building for the Future

The shift to Competency Based Education (CBE) calls for modern, well-equipped learning spaces. To match our growing population and the demands of the new curriculum, we are expanding our infrastructure with new classrooms, sanitation facilities, a computer room, a library, and a home science room.

These spaces are designed to inspire curiosity, foster innovation, and give every learner the opportunity to develop applicable knowledge, attitudes, and skills for life and career.
