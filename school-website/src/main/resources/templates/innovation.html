The TFalls Foundation has been a transformative partner in Mt Angels' growth story. Through strategic investments in infrastructure, the Foundation is helping the school meet the demands of the Competency Based Curriculum and provide a world-class learning environment for over 1,400 learners.

TFalls 6-Classroom Storey Building

A modern two-storey classroom block to ease congestion and support CBC learning

Project Overview

The TFalls Foundation 6-Classroom Storey Building is a ground-plus-first-floor structure designed to provide modern, spacious learning spaces for Mt Angels' growing population. With over 1,400 learners and rising enrolment each year, the school urgently needed additional classrooms to ease congestion and meet CBC infrastructure standards.

The building houses 6 fully equipped classrooms — 3 on the ground floor and 3 on the first floor — built to modern standards with proper ventilation, lighting, and durable finishes.

TFalls Field

From 1.5 acres of government land to a proper sports and activities ground

TFalls Field — Mt Angels Comprehensive School

About TFalls Field

In May 2025, Laikipia County donated 1.5 acres of land to Mt Angels Comprehensive School, increasing the school's total size to approximately 3 acres. This expansion was critical — the school had long struggled with limited space for extracurricular activities, assemblies, and sports.

The TFalls Foundation stepped in to transform this raw land into TFalls Field — a levelled, usable sports and activities ground that gives learners the space they need for football, athletics, PE lessons, and school events.
