School Leadership Through the Years

From headteachers under the old 8-4-4 system to principals under CBC

Under the old 8-4-4 curriculum, the head of a primary school was called a Headteacher. With the introduction of the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) and the elevation of primary schools to comprehensive schools (including Junior Secondary), the title changed to Principal.

First Headteacher (Government-posted)

Madam Mercy Ngatia

Late 1990s – …

The first government-posted teacher who also served as headteacher. She laid the administrative foundation of Mt Angels during its earliest years as a formal primary school.
