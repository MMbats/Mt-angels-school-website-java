Our Teams

Four pillars that hold the school together

Board of Management

The BOM provides strategic oversight and governance for the school. Led by the Chairperson, the team ensures Mt Angels operates with integrity, accountability and a clear focus on the welfare of every learner.

Parents Teachers Association

The PTA bridges parents and the school, championing the partnerships that make our learners' success possible. From feeding programmes to infrastructure projects, the PTA is the heartbeat of the Mt Angels community.

School Administration

Led by the Principal, the administration is made up of the Deputy Principal, Senior Teachers and support staff. Together, they handle the day-to-day running of the school — from curriculum delivery to discipline, welfare and operations.

Heads of Department

Our HODs lead academic excellence across Mathematics, Languages, Sciences, Creative Arts and Pre-Technical Studies. They drive curriculum quality, mentor teachers and ensure every learner gets the very best instruction.
